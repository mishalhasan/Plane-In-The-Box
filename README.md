# The Aviation Jack-in-the-Box

An interactive 3D web experience framed inside a luxury aircraft In-Flight Entertainment (IFE) screen, built with **Three.js**, **TypeScript**, **Vite**, and procedural **Web Audio**.

## First Working Milestone Highlights

- **Aircraft IFE Bezel & Cabin HUD**: Midnight-navy, Qatar-inspired burgundy (`#5c0632`), champagne-gold (`#d8b56c`), and ivory (`#f7f4ed`) cabin aesthetic with live flight-phase telemetry, winding tension ring, 3D-projected crank callout, sound/mute toggle, and replay reset.
- **Tactile Mechanical Winding**: Wind the right-side 18-tooth brass ratchet crank via circular pointer drag, trackpad/mouse scroll, or holding `Space`, driving gear rotation, clicking pawl mechanics, music-box celesta chimes, cabinet tremor, and compass-rose glow up to `100%`.
- **Pop-Out & Aerobatic Launch**: At `100%`, the front clasp unlatches, the hinged lid springs open with damped physics, the coiled brass spring extends with a celebratory particle burst, and a custom 3D widebody jetliner launches into an aerobatic foreground swoop.
- **3D Volumetric Skywriting**: The aircraft climbs into the twilight sky and writes **"Qatar Airways"** across 24 spline-aligned strokes of normal-lit FBM cloud puffs with smooth $C^1$-continuous smoke-off 3D turnarounds between strokes.
- **Finale Salute & Replay**: Concludes with a sweeping hero flyby and wing-wave salute before settling on a balanced hero composition with one-click **Replay** back to `0%`.
